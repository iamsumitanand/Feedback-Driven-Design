# Aesthetic Direction Reference

Use this during Phase 1 to help the user commit to a clear aesthetic direction.
Pick one and name it in the spec. Don't blend more than two.

---

## Minimal / Editorial

**Character**: Generous whitespace. Type does the heavy lifting. Restrained palette.
**Fonts**: Freight Display + Neue Haas Grotesk / Playfair + Instrument Sans
**Colors**: Off-white or cream background, near-black text, one muted accent
**Motion**: Slow, deliberate fades. No bouncing.
**Good for**: Content-heavy, long-form, documentation, editorial, news

---

## Bento / Dashboard

**Character**: Information-dense grid of cards. Layered data. Status at a glance.
**Fonts**: DM Mono or JetBrains Mono for data, DM Sans for labels
**Colors**: Dark surfaces (#0F0F0F–#1A1A2E), glowing accents (green, teal, amber)
**Motion**: Number counters, subtle card hover lifts, live-data pulse
**Good for**: Analytics, admin panels, SaaS dashboards, monitoring tools

---

## Warm / Human

**Character**: Approachable. Slightly rounded. Earthy or warm tones. Feels handcrafted.
**Fonts**: Sohne or Lora, paired with a simple sans like Plus Jakarta
**Colors**: Warm beige, terracotta, dusty rose, soft sage — never cold grays
**Motion**: Gentle easing, soft hover glows, no sharp transitions
**Good for**: Consumer apps, health/wellness, community platforms, personal tools

---

## Brutalist / Raw

**Character**: No apologies. Heavy borders, clashing type, aggressive layout.
**Fonts**: Druk or Impact for display, monospace for body
**Colors**: High contrast — pure black/white with one neon or primary color
**Motion**: Instant. No easing. Or exaggerated, glitchy distortion.
**Good for**: Creative agencies, portfolios, events, music/arts platforms

---

## Luxury / Refined

**Character**: Slow animations. Fine lines. Gold or platinum accents. Nothing crowded.
**Fonts**: Cormorant Garamond, Optima, or Didot — nothing sans-serif in display
**Colors**: Ivory, deep navy or forest green, champagne or rose gold accent
**Motion**: Silky smooth reveals. Long duration. Staggered elegance.
**Good for**: Fashion, luxury goods, premium SaaS, hospitality, finance

---

## Futuristic / Sci-Fi

**Character**: Dark. Glowing borders. Monospace grids. Feels like a cockpit.
**Fonts**: Orbitron, Rajdhani, or Share Tech Mono
**Colors**: Deep black, electric blue or cyan glow, red alerts
**Motion**: Scan lines, data flicker, fast typewriter effects
**Good for**: Developer tools, cybersecurity, games, tech startups

---

## Playful / Toy-like

**Character**: Chunky elements. Bold fills. Slightly imperfect. Joyful.
**Fonts**: Nunito, Fredoka, or Baloo — rounded, friendly
**Colors**: Primary color pops on white or light yellow. No muted grays.
**Motion**: Bouncy spring physics. Exaggerated hover states.
**Good for**: Kids products, gamification, onboarding flows, consumer apps

---

## Retro / Nostalgic

**Character**: References a specific era. Intentionally dated in a cool way.
**Sub-styles**: 70s groovy, 80s neon, 90s web, Y2K chrome
**Fonts**: Era-specific pairings — Cooper Black (70s), VCR OSD Mono (90s), etc.
**Colors**: Era-specific — avocado green + brown (70s), hot pink + black (80s)
**Motion**: Depends on era — slow and organic (70s) vs flashy blink (90s web)
**Good for**: Music, fashion, food/beverage brands, nostalgia-driven products

---

## Organic / Natural

**Character**: Soft edges. Nature textures. Breathing room. No sharp angles.
**Fonts**: Lato, Nunito, or actual handwritten/script fonts
**Colors**: Sage, forest, stone, sand, sky — all desaturated, nothing bright
**Motion**: Slow, breathing animations. Things grow and sway, not slide and click.
**Good for**: Sustainability brands, wellness, food, travel, outdoor lifestyle

---

## Tip for Phase 1

Don't let the user say "modern and clean". That's not a direction.
Push them to pick one of the above, or describe a feeling: "feels like a high-end
magazine" → Luxury/Editorial. "Feels like a Bloomberg terminal" → Bento/Futuristic.

Lock it in the spec with one sentence:
> "Aesthetic direction: Warm/Human — terracotta and sage palette, rounded cards,
> soft hover transitions. Think Notion meets Airbnb."
